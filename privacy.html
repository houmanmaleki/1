Privacy Policy.

This is a starter privacy notice for the Senserio website and must be reviewed and updated to match the business’s actual practices before launch.

Owner review required: this template is not legal advice. Confirm actual collection, storage, retention, and service-provider practices before publishing.

1. Information you provide

If you contact Senserio by email or phone, you may provide contact details and information about a project. This static website does not store quote-form submissions on a server.

2. How information may be used

Information you choose to provide may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss project scope, prepare quotations, and communicate about related work.

3. Service providers and storage

When you open an email draft, information is handled by your device and email provider. If the website is later connected to a form service, this notice must be updated to describe that service and its data practices.

4. Your choices

You can choose whether to contact Senserio and what information to include. Avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for the enquiry.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact houman@senserio.ca.
